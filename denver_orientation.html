Class Requirements & Attendance Policy

Device & Check-In Policy

  • Laptop or Desktop Computer STRONGLY RECOMMENDED.
  • Your camera and microphone must be operational throughout the session.
  • You must remain visible on camera for the duration of the class.
  • Log in using the exact legal name shown on your state/government ID.
  • Join the waiting room with camera and microphone muted until instructed.

Learning Environment

  • NO MOVING VEHICLES: You cannot attend while operating or riding in a moving vehicle.
  • Must be in a quiet, indoor space free of background noise and distractions.
  • Distractions will result in removal from the session and require GM review.

Multiple Sign-Ups & Attendance Policy

  • Choose your date with care: Any duplicate or multiple submissions require administrative review.
  • Your first scheduled date is treated as your confirmed attendance expectation unless your trainer or store General Manager is notified in advance.
  • Enrollment closes strictly 2 hours before class start time.

Google Meet Link Delivery

  • Your class trainer (Richard) will email your Google Meet video link to you after the registration window closes.
  • Please check your email and spam/junk folder prior to class start.
  • Contact Richard (richard@team-wow.com) before class if you have questions.
